Governance & Decentralisation

Formed on 1 November 1956, the Government of Kerala has been a pioneer in decentralised governance. Through the three-tier Panchayati Raj system, local bodies like Thavinhal Grama Panchayat are empowered to address community needs directly. This WebGIS portal represents our commitment to transparency and modern planning through technology.
